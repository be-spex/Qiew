# Trail Notes

Hiking the **Harz** in late autumn: what to pack, where to stop and how the weather behaves above 1,000 m.

## Packing list

1. Waterproof shell and a warm mid-layer
2. Boots with a good grip – the roots are slippery
3. Thermos with something hot
4. Map, even if the phone works

> The summit is in the clouds on most days. Plan the view as a bonus, not as the goal.

## Stages

| Stage | Distance | Climb | Time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bad Harzburg – Torfhaus | 14 km | 560 m | 4 h |
| Torfhaus – Brocken | 9 km | 380 m | 3 h |
| Brocken – Schierke | 7 km | – | 2 h |

## Notes

- Check the [weather station](https://example.org/stations) before you start.
- The Brockenbahn runs all year; the last train down leaves at 17:20.
- Mind the `closed` signs near the bog: the boardwalk is repaired in winter.
